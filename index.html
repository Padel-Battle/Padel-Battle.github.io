Visit support ↗
Do I need an account?

No. Create a tournament and start playing without signing up. Player names, match results and tournament data are stored on your device.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Core tournament features work offline. Firebase receives technical analytics and crash reports when connected; see our Privacy Policy for details.

Can we keep the same partner?

Yes. Choose Pairs Americano or Pairs Mexicano to play with fixed partners and follow standings for pairs.

Is it available for my phone?

Padel Battle is available on iPhone and Android. The screens shown on this page are from the English iOS app.
